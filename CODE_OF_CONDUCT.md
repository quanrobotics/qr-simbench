# Code of conduct

QR-SimBench adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and
focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and
publishing others' private information are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@quanrobotics.org**. Reports are handled confidentially by the
QR maintainers, who may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
